Marble blocks

Raw marble blocks for processors and factories

Quarry blocks of Indian marble for gangsaw and block-cutter processing. Selected at the quarry, measured in cubic metres and photographed on every face before you buy.

Sourced from Rajasthan's marble quarries, shipped from Mundra and Kandla.

Selected at the quarry, not from a list

Buying blocks from abroad is mostly about trust in the selection. A block that looks clean on one face can carry a crack or a colour change through the middle.

We inspect blocks at the quarry face. We look for sound material, consistent colour, and a size that suits your saws. Each block is numbered, measured and photographed wet on all six faces, so you see the veining as it will appear once polished.

Our block customers are processing factories and slab producers who need a steady supply of Indian varieties.

Varieties

Marble varieties available as blocks

Availability depends on what each quarry is producing. We send a current block list on request.

Nirzerna White Marble

Pale ivory ground with soft, restrained veining. Our most requested white for floors, walls and vanity tops.

Aagaria Marble

Warm cream with grey-gold movement. Suits classical interiors, hotel lobbies and bathrooms.

Indian Onyx

Layered and translucent. Supplied resin-backed for backlit walls, bar fronts and feature panels.

Indian Green Marble (Verde Guatemala)

Deep forest green with white crystalline veining. Dense and hard-wearing for a marble.

Panda White Marble

Snow-white ground with bold black streaks. Book-matched for statement walls and islands.

Questions buyers ask

Frequently asked questions

How are marble blocks measured and priced?

Blocks are priced per cubic metre on their net measured dimensions (length, height, width). We send the measurements and photographs of every face before you commit.

How many marble blocks fit in a container?

Marble weighs roughly 2.7 tonnes per cubic metre, so a 20 ft container usually carries about 9 to 10 m³, often one or two blocks. We size and pair blocks to make good use of the payload.

Can I inspect the blocks before shipment?

Yes. You or your agent are welcome at the quarry or our yard. Otherwise we send wet-surface photos and videos of all six faces so veining and any defects are visible.

Do you supply gangsaw-size blocks?

Yes, subject to what the quarry is producing. Tell us your gangsaw or block-cutter dimensions and we will only offer blocks that fit.
