Marble slabs

Indian marble slabs, wholesale from Udaipur

Gangsaw and cutter slabs cut from Rajasthan's marble quarries. Polished, honed or leathered, packed in fumigated bundles and shipped by container to importers, fabricators and projects worldwide.

Udaipur, Rajasthan. Exporting natural stone for more than 25 years.

Slabs selected block by block

Udaipur sits in the middle of Rajasthan's marble belt, the region that produces most of India's marble. We buy blocks at the quarry and have them sawn into slabs under our supervision.

Each block is checked for cracks and colour consistency before sawing. Slabs from one block stay together and in cutting order. That lets you book-match a feature wall and keeps one floor in a single shade.

We supply importers and stone yards that stock slabs, fabricators cutting countertops and vanity tops, and contractors buying for a single project.

Our marbles

Indian marble varieties we export as slabs

These are the marbles we ship most. If you need a variety that isn't listed, tell us and we will source it.

Nirzerna White Marble

Pale ivory ground with soft, restrained veining. Our most requested white for floors, walls and vanity tops.

Aagaria Marble

Warm cream with grey-gold movement. Suits classical interiors, hotel lobbies and bathrooms.

Indian Onyx

Layered and translucent. Supplied resin-backed for backlit walls, bar fronts and feature panels.

Indian Green Marble (Verde Guatemala)

Deep forest green with white crystalline veining. Dense and hard-wearing for a marble.

Panda White Marble

Snow-white ground with bold black streaks. Book-matched for statement walls and islands.

Questions buyers ask

Frequently asked questions

Which thicknesses do you supply marble slabs in?

18 mm and 20 mm are standard for flooring and wall cladding, and 30 mm for countertops, vanity tops and stair treads. Other thicknesses can be cut to order.

Can I see the actual slabs before they are shipped?

Yes. Before loading we send photographs of the slabs from your block, numbered in sequence, so you approve the material you will receive rather than a sample.

Do you supply book-matched marble slabs?

Yes. Slabs from one block are kept in cutting order, so consecutive slabs can be book-matched or vein-matched on a wall or island.

What is the minimum order?

We ship by the container. Different stones and thicknesses can usually be combined in one container, which suits importers building a range.

Which countries do you ship marble slabs to?

We export to importers, fabricators and project buyers in the USA, the UK, Europe, Australia, New Zealand and the Middle East.
