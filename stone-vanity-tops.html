Marble & Stone Vanity Tops

Cut-to-size luxury vanity tops in Indian marble and granite, with integrated basins, undermount cutouts and edge profiles worked to your drawing.

MINISTRY OF COMMERCE

Cut to your drawing, not to a catalogue

Vanity tops are a cut-to-size product, which means the drawing governs everything. You send dimensions, cutout positions, tap hole centres and the edge profile, and we produce against that. For repeat programmes we hold the veining direction consistent across shipments so a fifty-room hotel floor does not arrive looking like fifty different stones.

We supply in 18mm, 20mm and 30mm as standard, with other thicknesses on request, and we can supply a matched basin from the same block where the design calls for it. Where a project calls for a matched luxury vanity unit, we supply the top and basin cut from the same block.

SPECIFICATION

Fabrication to specification

  • Thicknesses: 18mm, 20mm and 30mm as standard. Other thicknesses on request.
  • Basin arrangement: integrated basin carved from the same block, undermount cutout with polished reveal, or vessel-ready flat top with tap holes only.
  • Edge profiles: worked to your drawing.
  • Cutouts: basin apertures, tap hole centres and waste positions cut to your dimensions.
  • Backsplash and upstand: supplied cut from the same block on request, so the veining carries across.

Send a dimensioned drawing or template with cutout positions and tap hole centres. We will confirm against block size and quote on that basis.

KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can the basin be carved from the same block as the vanity top?

Yes. An integrated basin is cut from the same block so the stone and veining run continuously from the top into the bowl. We also supply tops with undermount cutouts or as flat vessel-ready tops with tap holes only.

Q. What thicknesses do you supply?

18mm, 20mm and 30mm as standard, with other thicknesses available on request.

Q. How do we send you our dimensions?

Send a dimensioned drawing or template showing overall size, cutout positions, tap hole centres and the edge profile. We confirm against available block sizes before quoting.
