One block, one tub

A stone bathtub is the largest single piece we carve. It comes out of one block, which means the block has to be sound the whole way through and large enough to lose most of its mass to the hollow. We select for that at the quarry, not at the factory.

The result is a tub that holds heat far longer than acrylic or cast iron and reads as one continuous piece of stone. These are specified most often for hotel suites, spas and private residences, and they are quoted per piece against your drawing.

For architects and interior designers

A centrepiece for ultra-luxury bathrooms

A tub carved from a single block is rare and heavy, and every one is unique. Designers specify them where the bathroom is meant to be remembered.

We work from your drawings or a reference image. Before any carving starts, we propose blocks in your chosen stone and send photographs, so you and your client approve the actual stone that will become the tub.

Every tub is carved and hand-finished in Udaipur. We send progress photos, then ship it in a braced, fumigated crate to your port with full export documents.

Weight and access need planning early. Read our guide to specifying single-block marble bathtubs for weights, floor loading, finishes and care.

How a commission works

From drawing to installed tub

  1. BriefDrawings or a reference, the stone you have in mind, and the project location.

KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the bathtub carved from a single block?

Yes. Each tub is hollowed from one block of stone. Nothing is bonded or assembled, so the veining runs continuously around the form.

Q. How much does a stone bathtub weigh?

A freestanding marble tub around 170 cm long often weighs 0.8 to 1.5 tonnes empty, depending on the stone, shape and wall thickness. We supply the exact figure with your quotation. Confirm your floor loading capacity and access route before specifying.

Q. Can you carve a tub to our own design?

Yes. Send a dimensioned drawing and we will confirm what is achievable against available block sizes in your chosen stone.

Q. Who buys single-block marble bathtubs?

Mostly architects, interior designers and developers working on luxury residences, villas, spas and hotel suites, as well as importers who supply them.

Q. How should a marble bathtub be cared for?

Use pH-neutral cleaners only, rinse after use and re-seal the stone periodically. Acidic products, including bath bombs with citric acid, will etch marble.

Q. Can you ship a single bathtub abroad?

Yes. A tub ships in its own braced, fumigated crate, either in a full container or as shared-container cargo through your freight forwarder.
